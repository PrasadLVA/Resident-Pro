Garbage Segregation
Mandatory 4-bin waste segregation as per Solid Waste Management (SWM) Rules, 2026 — effective 1 April 2026, replacing the 2016 rules
⚖️
NEW LAW — SWM Rules 2026 (w.e.f. 1 April 2026) India has upgraded from a 3-bin to a mandatory 4-bin system. The old Pink Bin (Reject Waste) has been split into two separate streams — Sanitary Waste and Special Care Waste. Non-compliance attracts penalties under the Polluter Pays Principle.
🟢
GREEN BIN
Wet / Organic Waste
No plastic liner
What Goes In
  • •Vegetable & fruit peels
  • •Cooked / leftover food
  • •Egg shells, meat & fish bones
  • •Rotten fruits & vegetables
  • •Tea bags, coffee grinds
  • •Flowers, garden & leaf waste
  • •Tissue soiled with food
  • ⚠️Coconut shells — keep SEPARATE, not in bin
Disposal: Composting or bio-methanation at nearest facility
🔵
BLUE BIN
Dry / Recyclable Waste
Rinse if soiled
What Goes In
  • •Plastic: bags, bottles, boxes, wrappers, toys
  • •Paper: newspaper, magazines, cardboard, pizza boxes, Tetrapaks
  • •Glass: UNBROKEN bottles & jars only
  • •Metal: cans & containers
  • •Wood & Rubber items
  • Always rinse soiled items before placing
Disposal: Sent to Material Recovery Facilities (MRF) for sorting & recycling
🔴
RED BIN
Sanitary Waste
Wrap in newspaper first
What Goes In
  • •Diapers & baby wipes
  • •Sanitary napkins & pads
  • •Tampons & condoms
  • •Bandages & used tissues
  • •Hair, ear buds, nails
  • •Swept dust & animal waste
  • •Broken glass (wrap in newspaper)
  • •Razors, blades, used syringes
Disposal: Securely wrapped — separate collection & special disposal
⚫
BLACK BIN
Special Care Waste
Authorised disposal only
What Goes In
  • •Expired medicines & pills
  • •CFL & LED bulbs, tube lights
  • •Batteries (all types)
  • •Mercury thermometers
  • •Paint cans & chemical containers
  • •Wires, CD/DVD, electronic toys
  • •All other E-waste
Disposal: Hand over ONLY to authorised agencies or designated collection centres — never in common bins
🔄
What Changed from the Old 3-Bin System?
OLD Pink Bin (Reject) has been split into two — Sanitary items (Red Bin) & Special Care/Hazardous items (Black Bin) now have separate streams
OLD Blue Reusable Bag is now a Blue Bin — same recyclables, but officially a 4th bin stream, not a bag
E-waste & batteries now go in the Black (Special Care) Bin — no longer in a separate E-waste drop box only
Large apartments (>20,000 sq ft) are now Bulk Waste Generators — must register on CPCB portal & process wet waste on-site
🏢
Is Your Apartment Complex a Bulk Waste Generator (BWG)?
Your complex qualifies as a BWG if it has built-up area ≥ 20,000 sq ft, or generates ≥ 100 kg of waste/day, or consumes ≥ 40,000 litres of water/day. Most mid-to-large apartment complexes qualify. As per SWM Rules 2026, BWGs are required to: (1) Register on the CPCB centralised online portal, (2) Process wet waste on-site via composting / bio-methanation, or obtain an EBWGR certificate if on-site processing is not feasible, (3) Submit annual returns by 30 June each year, (4) Face environmental compensation fines for violations under the Polluter Pays Principle.

📌 Bengaluru-specific: BSWML & KSPCB guidelines on the above process are yet to be finalized. The EBWGR certification process is still evolving. AWPs are currently uploading waste data on the BSWML portal. MC/ECs should monitor updates from GBA / BSWML / KSPCB.

✗

⚖️
Legal Mandate: Waste segregation is mandatory by law under the Solid Waste Management Rules, 2026 (MoEFCC, Govt. of India, effective 1 April 2026). Fines are levied by the local municipal body — in Bengaluru, this is now the Greater Bengaluru Authority (GBA), which replaced BBMP with effect from 15 May 2025 under the Greater Bengaluru Governance Act, 2024.

⚠️ Implementation Note for Bengaluru Apartments: While the SWM Rules 2026 mandate registration on the CPCB centralised portal and annual returns by 30 June, the BSWML (Bengaluru Solid Waste Management Limited) and KSPCB (Karnataka State Pollution Control Board) are yet to release detailed operational guidelines on how this will work in practice for housing societies in Bengaluru. Authorised Waste Processors (AWPs) are currently uploading waste pick-up data on the BSWML website. The process around EBWGR (Extended Bulk Waste Generator Responsibility) certificates is still evolving and no finalized procedure has been notified as of May 2026. MC/ECs should watch for updates from GBA, BSWML and KSPCB, and consult their local ward office for current ground-level requirements.

Water Leakage Between Flats
A clear policy on who bears the cost of inter-flat seepage repairs
🏗️
Builder's Defect
If the leakage is due to original construction — cost is shared 50:50 between the two flat owners.
50 : 50 split
🔨
Custom Modification
If the leakage is entirely due to a modification done by one flat owner — that owner bears 100% of the repair cost.
100% by modifier
⚖️
Mixed Cause
If both original construction and custom modifications contributed — cost is split 75:25 between the two flat owners.
75 : 25 split

Frequently Asked Questions
Answers to the most common questions from residents across all community sizes
What is the Association and who runs it?⌄

The Residents' / Owners' Welfare Association is a registered body of all apartment owners. It is governed by an elected Managing Committee (MC) of typically 15–20 members chosen at the Annual General Body Meeting (AGM). The MC manages day-to-day operations, enforces bye-laws, maintains facilities, and handles finances on behalf of all residents.

Who can vote in Association elections?⌄

Only registered apartment owners in good standing (with no dues outstanding for more than 30 days) can vote. Tenants and deemed members (authorized family members) generally do not have voting rights. For jointly owned apartments, typically only the primary owner (first name on the deed) can vote, unless they have authorized another joint owner in writing.

Can apartments be used for commercial purposes?⌄

No. Residential apartments are strictly for residential use only. Running a business, office, guest house, service apartment, or any commercial activity from a residential apartment is not permitted without the express written permission of the Managing Committee. Violations can lead to service withdrawal and legal action.

What are the quiet hours in the community?⌄

Quiet hours are typically 10 PM to 6 AM daily. During these hours, residents must keep noise levels minimal — including music, TV, conversations and musical instruments. For interior work, there is an additional quiet period of 1:30 PM – 3:30 PM during which no loud-noise work is permitted.

How do I log a maintenance complaint?⌄

Log complaints through the Association's online resident portal or by calling the Complaint Cell (available 24/7). You will receive a ticket number — keep this for reference. For escalations, email the MC directly and include the ticket number. The Association office handles administrative queries during office hours.

What happens if I'm locked out of my apartment?⌄

For safety reasons, security staff cannot enter your apartment through the balcony to open it. Keep a spare key with a trusted friend or relative as a precaution. In genuine emergencies (gas leak, water leak, medical emergency), the MC may authorize security to break open the lock if you are unreachable at your registered contact numbers.

Can I have guests using community facilities?⌄

Yes, occasionally. Guests must be accompanied by the resident at all times while using any facility. Guests are not permitted to use facilities independently. They must follow all the same usage guidelines, dress codes and time limits as residents.

How far in advance do I need to inform the Association before moving?⌄

You must submit the Move-In or Move-Out form to the Association Office at least 5 working days before your planned moving date, during office hours. The Association needs time to process your documents, collect fees, and issue the Gate Pass for your movers.

What fees are payable during move-in or move-out?⌄

Two fees are typically applicable: (1) a non-refundable Moving Fee to cover administrative and common area costs, and (2) a refundable Security Deposit (typically via an undated cheque) which is returned after a post-move inspection confirms no damage to lifts, lobbies or common areas. Exact amounts are set by the Association.

Which lift can I use on moving day?⌄

Only the Service Lift may be used for moving goods and furniture. You must collect the Lift Key and Protection Mats from Security before using it, and return them after your move for the inspection report. The Service Lift is unavailable from 9:30–11:30 AM daily due to garbage collection — plan accordingly.

As an owner, what do I need to do when my tenant changes?⌄

Inform the Association as soon as possible. Ensure the outgoing tenant submits the Move-Out form and clears all dues. The new tenant must submit the Move-In form with your authorization letter, lease agreement, and their valid ID. They must also apply for parking stickers, biometric access, and be added to the resident e-groups.

When is my security deposit refunded?⌄

After your move is complete, report back to Security who will conduct a physical inspection of the lift, lobbies and common areas you used. If no damage is found, Security will submit a clearance report to the Association Office and your security deposit cheque will be returned. If damages occurred, repair costs will be deducted.

How do I get a vehicle parking sticker?⌄

Fill in the Vehicle Parking Sticker Form at the Association Office. Bring your vehicle registration copy, sale deed or lease agreement, valid photo ID, and the applicable sticker fee. The Association will issue color-coded stickers for your vehicle(s) and parking slot.

I changed my vehicle. What do I do?⌄

Surrender the old parking sticker for that slot to the Association Office and apply for a new sticker using the Vehicle Parking Sticker Form with your new vehicle's registration details. Each slot can have only one valid sticker — duplicates attract heavy fines.

How long can a visitor park their vehicle?⌄

Visitors may park in designated visitor parking zones for up to 24 hours with a Visitor Parking Pass displayed on the dashboard. For stays beyond 24 hours, a Temporary Parking Pass must be applied for at the Association Office (subject to availability and additional charges).

Can I rent out my parking space to another resident?⌄

An owner may allow another resident to use their parking space by assigning their "right to use" — but this is subject to the owner's continued ownership of the apartment. If the apartment is sold, the assignment automatically ends. Car parking spaces cannot be sold separately from the apartment.

How are additional Association-owned parking slots allocated?⌄

Additional community parking slots (owned by the Association) are allotted through a transparent lottery process conducted once every two years. All eligible residents can apply. The lottery winner signs a rental agreement and pays advance rent. Subletting of such slots is strictly prohibited.

How much in advance must I apply for interior work permission?⌄

You must submit the Interior Work Permission Form to the Association Office at least 30 days before you plan to start work. The MC will review and respond within 15 days. If no response is received within 15 days, it is deemed approved unless the work poses safety or structural risks.

Can I work on Sundays or public holidays?⌄

No. Interior work is strictly prohibited on Sundays and all public holidays. Permitted work hours on all other days (Monday–Saturday) are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM. No loud-noise work is allowed during the daily quiet period of 1:30–3:30 PM.

Can I break a wall or modify the structure of my apartment?⌄

Beams, columns and load-bearing walls must never be tampered with. If any structural changes are planned, you must engage a qualified structural engineer to certify that the modification is safe, and submit this certificate with your permission form. Core cutting through beams (for A/C or exhaust) is not permitted.

Who is responsible for disposing of construction debris?⌄

The apartment owner is fully responsible for arranging debris disposal. Your contractor must remove debris from the premises. You must never dump debris in lobbies, basements, common areas or parking slots. For small loads of debris, you may contact the Association Office for a paid disposal service subject to availability.

What is the security deposit for interior work and when is it refunded?⌄

A refundable security deposit (via undated cheque) is collected before work begins to cover potential damages to common areas. Upon completion of work, report to the Association Office. The MC will inspect the apartment and common areas. If everything is in order and no unauthorized changes were made, the cheque is returned. Any damages or violations will be deducted.

Is waste segregation really mandatory?⌄

Yes. Under the Solid Waste Management (SWM) Rules, 2026 (effective 1 April 2026), waste segregation at source is mandatory for all citizens, institutions and housing societies across India. This replaces the 2016 rules. Non-compliance attracts fines under the Polluter Pays Principle — ₹1,000 and above in cities like Bengaluru, as levied by the local municipal body. In Bengaluru, this is now the Greater Bengaluru Authority (GBA), which replaced BBMP from 15 May 2025.

What are the four bins under the new SWM Rules 2026?⌄

🟢 Green Bin — Wet/Organic Waste: Food scraps, vegetable/fruit peels, cooked food, garden waste. Sent for composting or bio-methanation. No plastic liner.

🔵 Blue Bin — Dry/Recyclable Waste: Plastic, paper, glass (unbroken), metal, cardboard, rubber, wood. Sent to Material Recovery Facilities. Rinse if soiled.

🔴 Red Bin — Sanitary Waste: Diapers, sanitary napkins, tampons, condoms, bandages, used tissues, hair. Wrap in newspaper first. Separate collection & special disposal.

⚫ Black Bin — Special Care Waste: Medicines, bulbs/CFLs, batteries, paint cans, mercury thermometers, all e-waste. Hand over ONLY to authorised agencies — never in common bins.

What changed from the old 3-bin system?⌄

The old Pink Bin (Reject/Dry Waste) has been split into two separate streams: Sanitary Waste now goes in the Red Bin, and Special Care/Hazardous Waste (medicines, bulbs, batteries, e-waste) now goes in the Black Bin. The Blue Bag for recyclables is now an official Blue Bin. This gives us 4 bins instead of the earlier 3 bins + reusable bag system.

Is my apartment complex a Bulk Waste Generator (BWG)?⌄

Your complex qualifies as a Bulk Waste Generator if it meets any one of these criteria: (1) Built-up area ≥ 20,000 sq ft, (2) Waste generation ≥ 100 kg/day, or (3) Water consumption ≥ 40,000 litres/day. Most mid-to-large apartment complexes in India qualify. BWGs must register on the CPCB centralised online portal, process wet waste on-site (composting), and file annual returns by 30 June each year.

Where do medicines, bulbs and batteries go now?⌄

Under the new SWM Rules 2026, these are classified as Special Care Waste and must go in the Black Bin. They must be handed over only to authorised collection agencies or deposited at designated collection centres. Never put them in household bins, flush them, or throw them in common areas. Your MC/EC should coordinate with the local municipal body — in Bengaluru, the Greater Bengaluru Authority (GBA) (which replaced BBMP from 15 May 2025) — to identify authorised collection points. Note that BSWML & KSPCB are yet to release final operational guidelines for EBWGR certification in Bengaluru.

What tableware is allowed at community parties?⌄

Only reusable tableware (steel, melamine) or compostable alternatives (e.g., arecanut leaf plates and bowls) are permitted. Plastic, paper, thermocol and styrofoam plates, bowls, cups and cutlery are prohibited as per BBMP and SWM Rules. The security deposit for party hall use will not be refunded if disposables are found.

Do I need to register my pet with the Association?⌄

Yes. All pets must be registered with the Association Office using the Pet Registration Form. You must provide a photo of the pet, and a current anti-rabies vaccination record (mandatory for cats and dogs). Registration must be updated when vaccinations are renewed.

Is my dog required to be on a leash?⌄

Yes. Dogs must be on a leash at all times when in any common area — corridors, lobbies, gardens, grounds, and parking areas. Dogs must also wear an ID tag. Never allow dogs to roam off-leash.

What if my pet makes a mess in a common area?⌄

Pet owners or caretakers are fully responsible for cleaning up immediately. Pet pee in any indoor common area and pet poop in any common area (including lawns and grounds) are both considered violations and attract fines. Always carry a clean-up kit when walking your pet.

Can I use the elevator to bring my pet down?⌄

No. Pets must use the staircase, not the elevator/lift. This is for hygiene and the comfort of other residents who may share the lift.

How often is maintenance charged and how do I pay?⌄

Maintenance charges are typically billed quarterly. The Association sends a Demand Note via hand delivery and email. Payment can be made by cheque, demand draft, or NEFT bank transfer. When paying by NEFT, always mention your apartment number first in the transaction remarks so the Association can trace your payment.

What happens if I don't pay maintenance on time?⌄

If dues are unpaid for more than 30 days, you become a "Defaulting Member" — you lose voting rights and may lose access to facilities and services. After 3 months of default, the Association can deduct dues from your security deposit, rent out your parking slot and adjust income against dues, and may initiate legal proceedings.

I paid via NEFT but the Association doesn't show it as received. What do I do?⌄

Contact the Association accounts team with your bank transaction reference number, date, and amount. This is why it's important to always mention your apartment number in the NEFT transaction remarks — it makes it much easier for the accounts team to trace your payment.

As an owner, am I responsible for maintenance even if my tenant doesn't pay?⌄

Yes. The owner is ultimately responsible for all maintenance dues. Your lease agreement should include a clause binding the tenant to pay maintenance directly to the Association. However, if the tenant defaults, the owner must step in and pay. Include this in your lease agreement terms.

How is the Transfer Fee calculated when I sell my apartment?⌄

The Transfer Fee is typically calculated based on the super built-up area of the apartment (e.g., a per-square-foot rate set by the Association). It must be paid via Demand Draft in favor of the Association before a No Objection Certificate (NOC) is issued for the sale. The exact rate is specified in the Association's bye-laws — check with the Association Office.
